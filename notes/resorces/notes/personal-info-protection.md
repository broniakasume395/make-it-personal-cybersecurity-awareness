#Personal Information Protection
## 1. Protecting Private Date
* **Guard Personal Details:** Do not share sensitive details like your full birthdate, home address, or phone number on public platforms.
* **Check Account Privacy:** Set your social media profiles to private so only trusted friends can view your information.
## 2. Preventing Identity Theft
* **Be mindful of links:** Never click on random links asking you to verify your passwords or sensitive personal records.
* **Shred Documents:** Securely destroy physical documents containing identification before throwing them away.
