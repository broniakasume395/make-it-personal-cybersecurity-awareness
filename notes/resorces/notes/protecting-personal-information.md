# Protecting Personal Information

## Introduction

Personal information includes details such as full names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, passwords and banking information. This information is private and should be protected from unauthorised access, misuse and theft.

## Why Is Personal Information Important?

Personal information can be used to identify individuals, access accounts and conduct financial transactions. If it falls into the wrong hands, it may result in identity theft, financial loss, fraud and loss of privacy.

## Ways to Protect Personal Information

### 1. Use Strong Passwords
Create strong and unique passwords for different accounts. Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name or date of birth.

### 2. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication
Use additional verification methods to make it harder for criminals to access your accounts.

### 3. Limit Information Sharing
Avoid sharing sensitive information publicly or with people and organisations you cannot verify.

### 4. Be Careful with Emails and Links
Check messages carefully before clicking links or opening attachments. Criminals may use phishing to steal personal information.

### 5. Secure Your Devices
Use screen locks, update your software and install security updates regularly.

### 6. Use Secure Websites
Check that a website uses HTTPS before entering sensitive information. Avoid entering private details on suspicious websites.

### 7. Review Privacy Settings
Check your social media privacy settings and limit who can view your personal information.

## What Should You Never Share?

- Passwords and PINs.
- Banking details and card security codes.
- Personal identification numbers.
- Confidential account recovery information.

## Conclusion

Protecting personal information reduces the risk of identity theft, fraud and unauthorised access. Everyone should take responsibility for keeping their private information safe.
