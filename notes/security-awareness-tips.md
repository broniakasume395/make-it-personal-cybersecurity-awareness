# Safe Online Practices

The following practices can help individuals stay safe online.

1. Avoid clicking suspicious links in emails and messages.
2. Use trusted websites when sharing personal information.
3. Keep operating systems and applications updated.
4. Avoid downloading files from unknown sources.
5. Do not share passwords with other people.
6. Use multi-factor authentication where possible.

Following these practices reduces the risk of cyberattacks
and unauthorised access to personal information.
