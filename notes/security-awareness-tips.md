# Make It Personal: Security Awareness Tips

## Your Personal Information Matters

Protecting personal information is everyone's
responsibility. The following tips can help individuals
stay safe when using digital platforms.

## Cybersecurity Awareness Tips

### 1. Think Before You Share
Avoid sharing sensitive personal information such as
identification numbers, passwords, banking details
and private contact information with strangers.

### 2. Verify Before You Click
Always check suspicious emails, messages and links
before opening them. Criminals may use fake messages
to steal personal information.

### 3. Protect Your Passwords
Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts.
Never share your passwords with friends or strangers.

### 4. Be Careful on Social Media
Avoid publicly sharing personal details, your location
or information that criminals could use to identify you.

### 5. Report Suspicious Activities
Report suspected phishing messages, unauthorised
account access and other suspicious activities to
the appropriate security support team.

## Remember

Your personal information is valuable.
Think before you share. Stay alert. Stay protected.

**MAKE IT PERSONAL – Your Personal Information Matters.**
