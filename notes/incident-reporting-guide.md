# Incident Reporting Guide

## Introduction

A cybersecurity incident occurs when personal information, accounts or computer systems may have been accessed, exposed or affected without permission. Reporting incidents quickly can help reduce damage and protect information.

## What Should You Do If You Suspect an Incident?

1. **Do not panic:** Stay calm and avoid responding to suspicious messages.
2. **Secure your account:** Change your password immediately if you think it has been compromised.
3. **Enable multi-factor authentication:** Add an extra layer of protection to your account.
4. **Do not delete evidence:** Keep suspicious emails, messages, screenshots or other relevant details.
5. **Report the incident:** Contact the relevant organisation, institution or authorised IT support team.
6. **Monitor your accounts:** Check for unusual logins, transactions or changes.

## What Information Should You Report?

When reporting an incident, provide:

- A brief description of what happened.
- The date and time you noticed the incident.
- The affected account, device or information.
- Any suspicious messages, links or attachments.
- Any actions you have already taken.

## Important Reminder

Report suspected cybersecurity incidents as soon as possible. Never share your password or other confidential information when reporting an incident.
